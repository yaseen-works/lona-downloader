About Lona Downloader

Lona Downloader is a free online tool for saving videos and photos from the platforms you already use — TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, X (Twitter), Reddit, SoundCloud and YouTube. One tap on the golden circle fetches your link and saves the file to your device. No sign-up, no app install required, and no watermark added by us.

Why Lona exists

Most downloader sites are cluttered with pop-ups, fake buttons and forced redirects. Lona was built to be the opposite: a single clean screen that does one job well. It started in October 2026 as an independent one-person project and is maintained and improved continuously based on what real users ask for.

What Lona can download

  • TikTok videos in HD, without the watermark
  • Instagram reels and photos
  • Facebook and Reddit videos
  • Pinterest videos, X (Twitter) videos
  • SoundCloud audio

How it works

Copy a link from the app or site, open Lona, and tap the circle — the link is read from your clipboard automatically. Our server fetches the media from the platform and streams it to your device. Files are not kept on our server after your download finishes.

Free — and staying honest about it

Lona is free to use. Running costs are kept at zero-to-minimal by design so the tool can stay open to everyone, including students and small creators who repost their own work across platforms.
