How to Download TikTok Videos Without a Watermark

Last updated: 26 October 2026

TikTok's own save button does the job halfway: it hands you the video with TikTok's logo and the creator's username stamped across it, bouncing around the frame. That is fine for watching, but useless the moment you want a clean clip — for an edit, a reaction, or just a copy that looks like the original.

The good news is that the clean version already exists. TikTok's servers host an original copy of every video, without the logo and username overlay. A downloader that fetches that original file gives you exactly what was uploaded — nothing cropped, nothing edited, nothing covered up. That is what Lona does.

How to save a TikTok video without a watermark

  1. Copy the video link. Open the video in TikTok, tap Share, and choose “Copy link”. Short links work too — vm.tiktok.com and vt.tiktok.com are supported directly.
  2. Open the TikTok downloader and tap the golden circle. Lona reads the link you copied automatically — there is nothing to paste and no button to hunt for.
  3. Save your video. The clean video appears in Lona's VIDEOS library. Tap its Download button to save it to your device — in the Lona Android app it lands straight in your gallery.

That is the whole process. No TikTok login, no sign-up, no app to install if you are using the browser version — and it is free.

What to watch out for

  • Only public videos work. Private videos, deleted videos, and drafts cannot be fetched by any outside tool — if a site claims otherwise, it is not being honest.
  • Quality means “as uploaded”. Lona always picks the best version TikTok offers for that video. A video uploaded in low quality cannot magically become HD anywhere.
  • Creators still own their work. Download only videos you own or have permission to save, and credit the original creator when you share or repost.
  • Avoid tools that ask for your TikTok login. A downloader only needs the video's link. Anything asking for your account is asking for more than it should.
