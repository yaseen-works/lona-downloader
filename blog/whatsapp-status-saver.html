WhatsApp Status Saver: How to Save Statuses You've Viewed

Last updated: 26 October 2026

A friend posts a great status — a song, a moment, a quote you want to keep — and 24 hours later it is gone. The usual workaround is a screenshot, which crops the frame and destroys the quality, and does not work at all for videos. There is a better way, and it does not require anything shady: WhatsApp already stores the statuses you have viewed on your own phone. A status saver simply lets you keep the actual file.

How Lona's Status Saver works

Status Saver lives in the free Lona Android app. The design is deliberately strict: per-item save only. Nothing is auto-saved in the background, so your gallery never fills up with statuses you never wanted. You look at the list of statuses you have already viewed, pick one, tap save — and only that one lands in your gallery.

  1. View the status in WhatsApp first. Only statuses you have actually opened appear in the saver — that is how it is supposed to work.
  2. Open Status Saver in the Lona app. Your viewed statuses are listed newest first.
  3. Grant folder permission once. Android asks you to pick the statuses folder so Lona can read it. This permission is granted once, on your phone, and nothing leaves the device.
  4. Pick and save. Tap the status you want, tap save, and the original file — photo or video, in its full quality — is copied to your gallery.

Honest limits (read these before you expect magic)

  • Viewed statuses only. If you never opened a status, it is not on your phone and cannot be saved. No tool can fetch statuses you have not viewed.
  • Within the 24-hour window. Once a status expires and WhatsApp removes it from your phone, it is gone. Save it while it is still there.
  • No auto-save, by design. Lona will not silently copy every status you view. You choose each one — that keeps your gallery clean and your friends' content respected.
  • Personal use. Saving a status for yourself is one thing; reposting someone else's status publicly is another. Ask before you share something that is not yours.

A note on privacy

Everything happens on your phone. The saver reads files WhatsApp already stored on your device and copies the ones you choose to your gallery. Nothing is uploaded, and no modified WhatsApp is involved — your WhatsApp stays exactly as it is. It also works for WhatsApp Business, not just regular WhatsApp.

Frequently asked questions

Does Status Saver auto-save every status I view?

No — and that is deliberate. You pick each status you want to keep. Nothing is copied without your tap.

Can I save a status I never opened?

No. Only statuses you have viewed are stored on your phone, so only those can be saved.

Does the person know I saved their status?

WhatsApp shows the poster who viewed their status — that comes from the view itself. Saving the file on your own phone sends no extra notification.

Does it work for photos and videos both?

Yes — photo statuses save as images and video statuses as video files, in their original quality.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes, both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business are supported.
