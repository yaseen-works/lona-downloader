Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The short version

Lona Downloader has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not keep the files you download, we do not build a history of your links, and we never sell personal data — we barely collect any.

What happens when you download

  • The link you paste is sent to our server so it can fetch the media from the platform. It is processed in memory to complete your download.
  • Downloaded files are streamed to your device and are not stored on our server afterwards.
  • Your in-app video library lives only in your own browser or app storage on your device — it never leaves your phone.

Anonymous counters

We count totals only — how many visits the site received and how many downloads each platform produced — using an anonymous counting service. These counters hold no names, emails, IP profiles or per-user histories, and they cannot identify you.

Cookies

Lona does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store small functional data (such as your in-app library) locally on your device; you can clear it any time from your browser or app settings.

Third-party platforms

When Lona fetches media, the source platform (such as TikTok or Instagram) receives a normal request from our server, as it would from any tool. Your use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.

Copyright

Please download only content you own or have permission to save, and credit the original creator when you share. Rights holders can reach us via the contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a fresh date at the top.

Lona Downloader is not affiliated with TikTok, Instagram, Meta, Pinterest, X, Reddit, SoundCloud or YouTube.
